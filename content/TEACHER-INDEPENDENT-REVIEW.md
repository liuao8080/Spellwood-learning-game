Review date: 8 October 2026

Scope: the 48 questions in `src/teacher-questions.json`, including every prompt, answer choice, answer key, Chinese explanation and the six shared reading passages. This is a content review of an internal practice bank, not a claim of standardized-examination equivalence, CEFR calibration or certification.

Status: accepted for internal practice use after revision and independent recheck. No unresolved content blocker remains. All 48 questions have one defensible keyed answer under their stated instructions.

Reviewed source SHA-256: `401c77b34e55d2321230a3fb0fd4c9eeb1d7b823572c676e7744cfec45657345`.

The reviewed file contains 12 vocabulary, 12 grammar/usage, 12 complex-sentence and 12 reading questions. The reading questions share six passages of 100, 101, 102, 107, 108 and 110 whitespace-separated words. Each of the four answer positions is used 12 times. These mechanical checks supplement the item-level review below.

## Review criteria

- One defensible answer under the stated instruction and supplied text
- Natural academic English, with difficulty coming from vocabulary, structure and reasoning rather than specialist trivia
- Incorrect alternatives that reflect plausible misunderstandings without becoming additional correct answers
- Chinese explanations that accurately describe the English and do not add facts
- Reading conclusions confined to what the passages state or reasonably support

## Reading

The six short passages use self-contained academic and institutional situations. Their interpretation does not require outside historical, cultural or disciplinary facts. Each passage supports two questions. Their central distinctions cover access versus aggregate attendance, transparency versus a polished narrative, confounded comparisons, classification choices, evaluation of uncertain work, and respondent composition versus causal change. Selected alternatives were strengthened without changing the passages.

## Revisions verified

The review prompted correction of an adoption overclaim, an unsupported detail in a Chinese reading explanation, and several small scope or naturalness issues. The weakest grammar exercises were replaced or strengthened, and five reading items received more credible distractors. Every replacement was reviewed as a new complete item, including the revised explanation and all alternatives.

## Release interpretation

The review evaluates linguistic and inferential soundness. It does not establish empirical difficulty, item discrimination or suitability as a score-based proficiency certification. Those claims would require learner trials and calibration. Acceptance of content also does not certify the surrounding game’s rendering, persistence, matching or reward behavior.
